The Growth Story: Revenue Across Months & Markets

A visual narrative that transforms monthly revenue data into a clear story about growth patterns, regional performance, and business momentum.

View the visual story → GitHub repo

The story behind the numbers

Every business has a growth story, but the narrative often gets lost in spreadsheets and raw data. This visual story was created to bring revenue data to life — making the patterns, milestones, and regional dynamics instantly understandable to anyone looking at it.

The visual narrative answers three core questions that every business leader asks:

  • How are we growing overall? — The upward trend from January to December tells a clear story of momentum, with the line chart showing the journey month by month.
  • Where is our strongest market? — England stands out at $350K, telling a story about regional opportunity and market strength.
  • What's our baseline performance? — At $31K average revenue per month, there's a clear benchmark to measure against.

The narrative arc

This visual story is structured to guide the viewer from the big picture down to specific insights:

  • Opening frame: Total revenue of $3.06M immediately establishes scale and captures attention.
  • Building tension: The monthly line chart shows the journey — where growth happened, where it slowed, and the overall trajectory.
  • Key reveal: England's $350K contribution is highlighted, adding context about regional concentration.
  • Closing insight: The $31K average per month provides a reference point for evaluating future performance.

Visual design choices

  • Color hierarchy: The revenue line uses a bold color to draw attention to the growth trajectory, while England's bar is subtly highlighted to emphasize regional significance.
  • KPI cards: Three key numbers (total revenue, England revenue, average per month) are displayed prominently to anchor the story.
  • Sequential flow: The visual layout guides the eye from total revenue → monthly trend → regional breakdown → average insight.
  • White space: Clean, uncluttered design ensures the data speaks for itself without visual noise.
