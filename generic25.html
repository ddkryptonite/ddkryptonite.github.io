Average Revenue & Regional Performance Data Widget

Interactive Widget for tracking revenue trends across Australian states, with a 43% outperformance insight. Built in Power BI.

View live dashboard → GitHub repo

Project overview

This Power BI data widget visualizes revenue performance across Australian states from January 2024 to December 2025. It highlights the average revenue of $1.53M, year-over-year trends, and regional breakdowns — with a clear insight that the best year outperforms the average by 43%.

Built to help stakeholders quickly grasp regional disparities and seasonal patterns, the dashboard combines a time-series line chart, a state‑by‑state bar chart, and a high‑level KPI card.

Key metrics tracked

  • Average Revenue: $1,531,100 (January 2024 - December 2025)
  • Regional Performance: Victoria ($1.56M), Western Australia ($0.92M), South Australia ($0.53M), New South Wales ($0.03M), Tasmania ($0.02M)
  • Year-over-Year Trend: 2024 vs 2025 comparison with 43% outperformance
  • Insight: Best year outperforms average by 43%
